# Ambulance Situation Videos

Upload 4 camera videos here where at least one camera feed contains an **ambulance**:
- `camera_01.mp4`
- `camera_02.mp4`
- `camera_03.mp4` (contains ambulance)
- `camera_04.mp4`

Note: You can place videos either in this folder or directly in `situations/ambulance_situation/`.
`run_ambulance_cctv.py` automatically checks both locations!
